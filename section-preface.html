Preface

First, I would like to acknowledge the Genome Education Partnership (GEP) for inspiration. They created a resource for learning about Eukaryotic gene structure using the UCSC Genome Browser (Laakso et al. 2017). Their focus is on the Drosophila genome. My focus is on humans. My hope is that skills learned within these pages and the saved sessions I created (and will continue to curate) can be used as a springboard for continued exploration of the human genome.
